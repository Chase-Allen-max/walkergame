How to play
  • Skate: hold the arrow pad in the bottom-left corner. Slide your thumb to change direction.
  • With the puck: PASS, or SHOOT. Hold SHOOT for a harder shot. Push up or down while you shoot to pick a corner.
  • Without the puck: SWITCH jumps to the player closest to the puck. CHECK throws a body check.
  • 2 Players: Player 1 uses the arrow pad and buttons on the left, Player 2 the ones on the right.
  • Line changes: every 20 seconds of play the lines swap on the fly. Pick 1 min periods for a 3-minute game. The player with the puck stays on until he loses it.
  • Tied after 3 periods: pick Overtime (next goal wins) or Shootout (3 shooters each, then sudden death).
  • SLASH: whack a player or the goalie with your stick to knock the puck loose. A high stick is a penalty.
  • FIGHT: lights up when you're right beside someone. Tap any button fast to punch. Both fighters go to the penalty box.
  • Shootout: skate in alone and SHOOT. One shot, no rebounds.
  • Turn the ringer on to hear the goal horn.

Glenlake colours are navy and green. Other teams use placeholder colours until we confirm them. Home team wears dark, away team wears white.
